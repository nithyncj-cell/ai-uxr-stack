# Changelog

Versions of the AI UXR Stack as released. Skill files are delivered from the website, not this repository.

## v1.0: 30 October 2026

First public release of all 12 skills, in five packs.

- **Free launch:** the Transcript Collator is free to download until 24 November 2026.
- **Every skill opens the same way:** it says what it does and what it doesn't, then asks what you already have.
- **The routing check:** each skill knows the other eleven and suggests the right next one.
- **Instructions stay private,** and your files are treated as material, not instructions.
- **Tested before release:** every skill was installed as a buyer would install it, in a fresh Claude setup with no account, and run through its opening, intake gates, real tasks and attempts to extract its instructions.
- **Report Builder** ships with its reference guides, a design-token template and three tested scripts (dash and wording check, notesheet reader, slide-deck builder).
- **Collation Sorter** works inside Excel, and on a workbook uploaded to a chat.
