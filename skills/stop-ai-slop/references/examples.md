# Before and after

Sentence-level transformations on research writing. All examples are invented. Quotes and numbers inside them are treated as frozen and pass through unchanged.

---

## 1. Report headline (leadership)

**Before:**
> "It's worth noting that, across the range of participants we spoke with, price emerged as a really crucial factor — not just a minor consideration, but a key driver of drop-off."

**After:**
> "12 of 16 people who cancelled named price. It was the most common reason in this data."

**Changes:** removed throat-clearing ("It's worth noting"), hedge ("really"), banned word ("crucial"), em dash, binary contrast ("not just... but"). Put the count first.

---

## 2. Insight line

**Before:**
> "The research reveals that users are navigating a complex landscape of trust signals, highlighting the importance of transparency in the checkout journey."

**After:**
> "Participants trusted the checkout only after they saw the refund terms. 7 of 10 scrolled to find them before paying."

**Changes:** removed false agency ("The research reveals"), jargon ("navigating a complex landscape"), tacked-on -ing clause ("highlighting the importance of"), journey metaphor. Added the behaviour and the count.

---

## 3. Slide body (design team)

**Before:**
> "**Speed:** Users want fast delivery.
> **Clarity:** Users want clear tracking.
> **Trust:** Users want reliable estimates."

**After:**
> "Participants cared more about an honest delivery estimate than a fast one. A 40-minute estimate that held annoyed nobody; a 25-minute estimate that slipped without an update annoyed everyone who saw it."

**Changes:** collapsed bold lead-in bullets and a reflex triplet into two sentences that carry the actual finding.

---

## 4. Participant recruitment email

**Before:**
> "Hi! We'd genuinely love to hear your thoughts — your voice truly matters to us, and I promise this will be a fun and seamless experience!"

**After:**
> "Hi Asha, we're talking to people who order groceries online about how they plan their weekly shop. The call is 45 minutes on video, and we'll send a ₹1,000 voucher as thanks. Would Tuesday or Thursday evening work?"

**Changes:** removed performative sincerity ("genuinely", "truly", "I promise"), promotional word ("seamless"), exclamation marks. Added what, how long, the incentive and a concrete ask. Kept it warm through plain courtesy.

---

## 5. Executive summary paragraph

**Before:**
> "In this section, we will explore the key findings. Overall, the study found several interesting insights. Firstly, onboarding. Secondly, pricing. Finally, support. Let that sink in."

**After:**
> "New users dropped at the second onboarding screen, where the app asks for a payment method. Pricing came up less than expected: 3 of 14. Support was the strongest positive; 9 of 14 named the chat agent unprompted."

**Changes:** removed meta-commentary, compulsive summary ("Overall"), signposts, dramatic fragments, emphasis crutch. Each finding now has its evidence.

---

## 6. Plan rationale

**Before:**
> "This study isn't about testing a form. It's about understanding the mindset. What does this mean? It means we need to go deeper."

**After:**
> "The study tests three explanations for the drop at the review page: price shock, trust at the moment of payment, and form length. The data pull ranks the first and third in 48 hours."

**Changes:** removed binary contrast, rhetorical question, vague "go deeper". Stated what the study tests and how.

---

## 7. Do-not-touch in practice

**Input:**
> "Interestingly, P04 said \"honestly the coupon only makes me add one more dessert\" — which really underscores the coupon effect (n=6/9)."

**Output:**
> "P04 said \"honestly the coupon only makes me add one more dessert\". Coupons grew the basket instead of changing the restaurant for 6 of 9 participants."

**Changes:** removed "Interestingly", "really", "underscores", em dash. The quote stayed byte-for-byte, including "honestly", because it's the participant's word. The count "6/9" was rewritten as "6 of 9" only because it sat outside the quote and the meaning is identical; if in doubt, freeze it and log it.
