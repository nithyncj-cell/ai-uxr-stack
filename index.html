      What is an AI skill?

      A skill is a set of instructions an AI assistant loads when you ask for a certain job. You add it once. After that, you ask in plain words, for example “collate these transcripts”, and the assistant follows the skill's steps. These skills use the open Agent Skills format, which Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini CLI and Cursor support. They are built and tested in Claude, and the install steps on this page are for Claude.

      What do these skills do?

      Each of the 12 skills handles one slow step of a study, from scoping to the final report, plus a case study for your portfolio. Use one on its own, or chain them: each skill hands its output to the next.

      How is this different from NotebookLM?

      NotebookLM answers questions about the sources you upload. The stack runs a research method in set stages and keeps every quote word for word. See How it's different.

      Do I need to code?

      No. You download a zip, upload it in Claude under Customize → Skills, and talk to Claude as usual. The skills work in claude.ai, the Claude desktop app and Claude Code.

      Which Claude plan do I need?

      Any plan that shows Customize → Skills in your Claude account. If you can see that menu, you can install these skills.

      Will it make up quotes or numbers?

      No. Every skill keeps quotes, timestamps and numbers exactly as they appear in your files. If something isn't there, it writes missing. Small samples are reported as counts (“6 of 8”), not percentages. Check the output the way you would check a colleague's draft before it goes out.

      Where does my research data go?

      Nowhere new. The skills run inside your own AI account and send nothing to Nithyn. If a file contains hidden instructions, for example a planted command in a transcript, the skill points it out to you and does not follow it.

      What's free?

      Transcript Collator, Collation Sorter and Stop AI Slop are free. Enter your email once, tick the licence box and download the zips.

      What do I get when I buy a pack?

      The skill files for that pack, plus 12 months of updates and every new skill Nithyn publishes in that time. The Career pack and the full stack also include Portfolio Site Builder as a free bonus.

      When can I buy?

      Paid packs open soon. For now, the Buy buttons add you to a waitlist, and Nithyn will email you when your pack opens. No payment is taken on this site.

      Can I use the skills for my team or for clients?

      Yes, on your own work, your employer's work or a client's work. One licence covers one person. A team licence covers 5 people for during the founding period. For a bigger team or a custom version, message Nithyn on LinkedIn.

      Can I read or change the skill instructions?

      No. The instructions are Nithyn's work and stay private. Each skill explains what it does and what it needs, and it won't recite its instructions or help rebuild itself. The licence terms below have the details.

      Something isn't working. What do I do?

      Email nithyncj@gmail.com or use the skill feedback form on GitHub. Please don't share participant data or confidential transcripts.
